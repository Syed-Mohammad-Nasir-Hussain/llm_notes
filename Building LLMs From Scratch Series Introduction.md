## 🕰️ Evolution of Language Models

### Early Era → ELIZA (1960s)

One of the earliest chatbot systems.

#### Characteristics

- Rule-based
- Scripted responses
- No understanding
- No learning capability

**Example Flow**

```text
User asks question
       ↓
Generic response
       ↓
Conversation feels repetitive
```

---

### Modern Era → ChatGPT & LLMs

Modern systems can:

- ✅ Understand context
- ✅ Generate useful responses
- ✅ Recommend resources
- ✅ Produce structured output

### Progress Driven By

- Machine Learning
- Neural Networks
- Deep Learning
- Transformer Architecture

---

## 🔓 Open Source vs 🔒 Closed Source Models

### Open Source Models

Example:
- Llama family

Characteristics:

- Architecture visible
- Public access
- Community contributions
- Easier learning

Benefits:

- Transparency
- Faster research
- Better education

---

### Closed Source Models

Examples:

- Proprietary commercial models

Characteristics:

- Internal implementation hidden
- Limited architectural visibility
- API access

Benefits:

- Controlled deployment
- Commercial protection

---

## 📌 Important Observation

Open-source models are improving rapidly and approaching closed-model performance.

### Meaning

You can now learn advanced AI concepts publicly.

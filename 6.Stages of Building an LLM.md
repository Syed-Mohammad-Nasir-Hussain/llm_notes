## Tokenization

Definition:

> Breaking sentences into smaller units called tokens.

Example:

```text
Building LLMs
↓
Build
LLM
```

Purpose:

Convert language into machine-readable form.

---

## Vector Embeddings

After tokenization:

```text
Words
↓
Vectors
```

Goal:

Capture semantic meaning.

Example:

```text
Apple
Banana
Orange
```

↓

Similar vectors

---

Another example:

```text
King
Man
Woman
```

↓

Semantic relationships preserved

---

## Positional Encoding

Words must preserve order.

Example:

```text
Dog chased cat
```

≠

```text
Cat chased dog
```

Goal:

Teach the model sequence information.

---

## Data Batching

Training happens efficiently by grouping data.

Process:

```text
Dataset
↓
Mini Batches
↓
Model
```

Benefits:

- Faster Training
- Lower Memory Usage
- Better Optimization

## LLMs Changed NLP

## Two Major Training Stages

```text
Pre-training
↓
Fine-tuning
```

---

## Pre-training

Characteristics:

- Huge Dataset
- Unlabelled Data
- Foundation Model

---

## Fine-tuning

Characteristics:

- Labelled Data
- Specific Tasks
- Production Ready

---

## Transformer Secret

```text
Attention
=
Power
```

Attention gives:

- Context Awareness
- Long-Term Dependencies
- Better Prediction

---

## GPT vs Transformer

Transformer:

```text
Encoder
+
Decoder
```

GPT:

```text
Decoder Only
```

---

## Emergent Behavior

LLMs trained for:

```text
Next Word Prediction
```

But can perform:

- Translation
- Summarization
- Classification
- Question Answering
